Time Twist v81 — consistency audit

Scope: reviewed all 1,304 English dialogue records and all 721 menu labels for repeated names, locations, items, commands and stock responses. Checked Japanese context for the changed location references. This is a terminology review, not a new exhaustive bilingual translation audit.

Applied changes:
- T25/g2/r4: manor -> mansion
- TT1B/g3/r6: The devil -> The Devil
- TT5/g0/r16: In the hut! -> In the shack!
- TT5/g3/r16: manor -> mansion
- TT6C/g2/r19: Baron's -> baron's
- TT2/g3/r14: prison -> jail
- TT2 menu 65: Basement -> Cellar
- T22 menu 32: Prison -> Jail
- TT6B menu 30: Horse -> Mare

Meaningful distinctions retained:
- The modern museum basement is a separate location from the French cellar.
- Jailer is a person, Cell is a room, and Jail is the building.
- Generic devils and the idiom "the devil's own luck" retain lowercase.
- Speaker labels such as Old Man remain title case; menu commands remain sentence case.
- Pendant/medal describe the amulet's shape; Bell/Jar abbreviate the full artifact names.

Skipped because their text sections are full:
- TT2 menu Official -> Guard: requires 140 bytes, has 139.
- T22 menu Remove -> Take off: requires 120 bytes with the Jail change, has 119.
- The previously noted "save her" -> "save that girl" clarification remains skipped.

The alternative Jail -> Prison direction also exceeded the TT2 menu capacity (140 versus 139 bytes), so the shorter Jail was standardized throughout France.

Save migration:
- Converted the supplied slot 6 state for the combined v81 ROM.
- Replaced eight bytes of its resident T22 bank and updated the embedded ROM filename.
- Preserved all progress flags, CPU registers, internal RAM, disk overlays, audio, and PPU state.
- Existing menu record start addresses are unchanged in the resident bank.
- Full emulator loading and PPU playtesting were not performed.
