TIME TWIST v78 — GREEK MOVE RESPONSE

TT4/g1/r21
Before: I shouldn't move him.
After:  I'd better stay put.

This is a conditional refusal of the player's Move command. The Japanese
うかつに うごかないほうがいい refers to the speaker moving, not moving another person.

All other v77 dialogue, menu labels and gameplay scripts remain unchanged.
