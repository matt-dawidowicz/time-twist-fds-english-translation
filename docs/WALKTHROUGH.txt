# Time Twist: Rekishi no Katasumi de

## Complete English Walkthrough

This walkthrough has been revised for the current English translation patch (v83; earlier v82 terminology is retained).

Menu commands, objects, character names, locations, and puzzle answers use the English wording displayed by the patch wherever that wording is known. Directions such as “walk left” refer to direct character movement rather than menu selections.

The optional `Info` steps below show the identity of the person or animal you currently inhabit. The option is tied to specific locations, so it does not appear in every chapter or room.

---

# PART 1 — ZENPEN

## Prologue

Answer the opening multiple-choice/personality questions however you like. Despite appearances, the Fortune-Telling Service Center does not score the questionnaire: your blood type alone determines the displayed personality profile; your birth month has no effect, and the Yes/No answers only change which statements appear next. All twelve birth months appear on one menu. Continue until the time shift occurs and the main game begins.

## Mill and confrontation

The Gypsy Amulet is the pendant Frankie gave you; it appears as `Amulet` in the menu. This sequence begins after `Move → Watermill`.

### At the watermill

`Use → Amulet` — Simon notices it, and you explain that rust obscures its inscription. Do this before finishing the conversation below.
`Talk` — Simon wishes he could turn back time.
`Talk` — Simon discusses Einstein. A woman interrupts and holds you at gunpoint. Advance through all of this dialogue until the command menu returns.
`Look → Area` — A car approaches. The woman hands you and Simon over to Schmidt, and the scene changes to the car.

If you have already talked to Simon or used the amulet, some of these exchanges will already be complete. The checkpoints above identify when to continue; repeating a completed action does not restart its dialogue.

### In Schmidt's car

`Talk → Schmidt` — Schmidt reassures Simon that he will not have to build another bomb.
`Talk → Schmidt` — Schmidt gives the password and explains the escape plan. Advance the ensuing dialogue and attack scene until the combat menu appears.

### The confrontation

`Fight` — Schmidt fires his pistol.
`Fight` — Simon throws his shoe.
`Fight` — Simon spits at Hitler.
`Fight` — Schmidt throws his knife.

Advance the response after each attack. After the knife attack, `Use` becomes available. If you have already made some attacks, continue from the corresponding point above rather than counting four more from your current position.

`Use → Amulet` — The amulet shines and hurts Hitler. The game then presents the next commands in sequence.
`Look → Amulet` — The rust falls away, exposing the inscription.
`Read → Text` — You start reading it, but are forced out of Cougar's body. Advance the scene until the `Look`, `Talk`, `Listen`, and `Move` menu appears.

### Reach Cougar

You are now speaking to Cougar from outside his body. Alternate talking and listening:

`Talk → Cougar` — You draw attention to the words on the amulet.
`Listen` — Cougar hears you and notices the amulet.
`Talk → Cougar` — You tell him to read the inscription aloud.
`Listen` — Cougar notices the words; your further instruction and his full recitation follow within this same exchange. Keep advancing the dialogue.

If you used `Listen` before drawing Cougar's attention to the inscription, you only hear the three men crying out. Resume the sequence with `Talk → Cougar`. Once you have told Cougar to read aloud and heard him acknowledge the amulet, `Listen` advances to the recitation.

After the recitation, continue through the automatic scenes: the men recover in the car, Schmidt discusses reaching the border, Hitler speaks with the Devil, and you are pulled into another time shift. No further command selections are needed before the Part 1 ending and “TO BE CONTINUED.”

# CIVIL WAR CONTINUATION — MEYER AND LINCOLN

## Meyer's property

### Serve the coffee

`Talk → Meyer` — He asks you to take coffee to the guards.
`Look → Wagon` — Find the coffee pot and cups.

These two steps can be done in either order. Both are needed before the pot becomes available under `Use`.

`Use → Pot` — Pour a cup of coffee.
`Move → Outside`
`Use → Coffee → Offer` — Give the coffee to the guards and advance their conversation.
`Move → Mansion`
`Talk → Meyer` — He gives you permission to rest upstairs. This conversation moves you into the guest room automatically.

If you have already poured the coffee, continue with offering it; pouring again only reports that the pot is empty. If you already served the guards, return to Meyer. Talking to Lincoln and George is optional.

### Search the study

`Move → Hallway`
`Move → Study`

`Look → Desk` — Discover the three drawers; this makes `Open` available.
`Open → Drawer → Third drawer` — It is locked. This unsuccessful attempt is necessary: it makes `Drawer` available under `Take`.
`Take → Drawer → Second drawer` — Pull out the middle drawer completely. You do not need to open it first.
`Look → Drawer → Third drawer` — Look through the gap and discover the whip and hood. Advance the entire revelation scene.

Opening the first or second drawer is optional. Removing the first drawer is also possible, but it does not reveal the third drawer's contents; you still need to remove the second. The third drawer remains locked. If you have already removed the second drawer, go straight to inspecting the third. Merely removing the second drawer does not complete the investigation: you must see the whip and hood.

### Eavesdrop from the stairs

`Move → Hallway`
`Move → Stairs`

`Listen` — Meyer notices you and asks what is wrong. This is the setup for finding a hiding place, not the private conversation yet.
`Look → Area` — Notice the thick, ornate pillar.
`Hide` — Hide behind the pillar.
`Listen` — Hear Meyer request an appointment as Lincoln's adviser.
`Listen` — Hear Lincoln refuse. Continue through the ensuing confrontation and the revelation about the guards' coffee.

`Move → Go up` — Return toward the upstairs hallway. Meyer calls Belle and George downstairs, and the next scene leads to the island and boat puzzle.

If you explored in a different order:

- Visiting the guest room or stairs before the study is allowed; return to the study and discover the whip and hood before trying to find the hiding place.
- `Look → Area` reveals the pillar only after both the study discovery and an encounter in which Meyer notices you. Looking too early only describes the landing; look again after both events.
- Before discovering the evidence, `Talk` or `Move → Get down` at the stairs can also make Meyer notice you. If that already happened, you can skip the first `Listen` and go straight to `Look → Area` after discovering the evidence.
- If the pillar is already found, choose `Hide`. If already hidden, continue with `Listen`.
- If you already heard Meyer's request, only the next `Listen` is needed. Once the confrontation is complete, further listening repeats dialogue; choose `Move → Go up`.
- Going upstairs before the confrontation is complete does not advance to the island. Return to the stairs and finish eavesdropping.

## Desert journey — find shelter

`Hold → Sleeve → Stick out tongue` — Draw Joseph's attention to your thirst. He checks the canteen and finds it empty.
`Look → Body` — Notice your exhaustion.
`Look → Body` — Kashim can no longer continue; advance the collapse scene.

`Smell → Area` — Detect hay and realize there is a dwelling nearby.

If `Look → Body` only repeats the exhaustion description, first use the sleeve/tongue interaction about water. If `Smell → Area` only describes dust, finish the collapse and the two conversations above, then smell again. Once you have detected hay, use the sleeve/shout interaction.

## The nomads' directions puzzle

At the tent:

`Listen` — Joseph calls to the occupants; three men emerge.
`Listen` — Hear their conflicting directions: right says east, middle says north, left says west.
`Listen` — Hear their statements about one another. This third conversation supplies the remaining clues; the old guide omitted it.

`Talk → Camel` — Ask about the hay.
`Talk → Camel` — The camel says he is honest.
`Talk → Camel` — Learn that one man always tells the truth, one always lies, and one is unreliable.

The truthful man is the one on the left. Choose:

`Move → West`

If Joseph tells you to wait, you have not yet heard both the men's directions and the camel's explanation of the three men. Complete those conversations and try again. Talking repeatedly to the camel before hearing the directions will not reveal his final clue.

## Bethlehem stable — talk to the animals

### Sheep's quiz — Bethlehem stable

`Walk` — Go to the Sheep.

Choose these answers in order:

1. What Eve ate after the serpent tempted her: `Fruit of wisdom`
2. The one God of Judaism: `Jehovah`
3. Country Moses led the Israelites out of: `Egypt`
4. King who built the Temple in Jerusalem: `Solomon`
5. Savior title meaning "anointed one": `Christ`

Complete all five successfully. The answer wording above matches the game's choices. In this stable sequence, passing the quiz is a prerequisite for finishing the meal and advancing the story.

### Win the mare's permission and eat

`Walk` — Go to the Mare.
`Look → Mare`
`Attack → Compliment → Mane` — Compliment her hairstyle. `Attack` is the command's actual label in this scene.
`Eat → Hay` — Keep advancing the exchange until Kashim takes a large mouthful and thanks her for the meal.

The first Cow conversation and the completed Sheep quiz are both required for this full meal. If you ate earlier and only got a short eating response, complete those conversations, return to the Mare, and choose `Eat → Hay` again. If she objects to eating without permission, use the mane compliment first.

`Walk` — Return to the Cow.
`Talk` — Her rambling conversation makes the now-full Kashim sleepy. Advance the transition through the child's birth.

If the Cow only repeats her feeling that something important is coming, the full meal is not complete. Return to the Mare after satisfying the requirements above and eat again.

# NATIVITY / FINAL HISTORICAL CONFRONTATION

## The visitors arrive

`Look → Baby` — Hear Joseph explain the baby's name.
`Look → Room` — Notice someone approaching; advance the visitors' arrival and introduction.
`Listen` — Continue through the offering, the discussion of the name, and the Devil's appearance and explanation.

The Baby examination triggers the arrival check. If `Look → Room` only describes the stable, examine the Baby first and then look around again.

## Confront the Devil

When the game presents the restricted commands:

`Look → Devil`
`Leap at → Devil`

Advance the full confrontation and its aftermath until the regular command menu returns. These are prompted steps within the scene; do not look for `Leap at` on the earlier stable menu.

## Return to your body and use the belt

`Look → My body` — Examine your original body and advance the scene in which you return to it.
`Look → Body` — Now inspect the body you inhabit; this reveals the Time Belt around your waist.
`Use → Time Belt` — Open the buckle and expose its controls.
`Use → Time Belt` — Set the date and return to 1995.

`My body` and `Body` are distinct targets at different stages. Complete the return-to-your-body scene before looking for the belt. If `Use` is unavailable, inspect `Body` after returning to it.
